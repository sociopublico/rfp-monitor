Skip to main content Requests for Proposals ShareFacebookTwitterLinkedInEmail The CDC Foundation issues requests for proposals (RFP) to sub-grant funds received for a specific project. These RFPs will seek:providers of products or services required for a specific CDC Foundation program with CDC or a public health partner.eligible nonprofit or governmental organizations that wish to participate in a CDC Foundation program addressing a specific health issue.a consultant/independent contractor (i.e. a sole proprietor, company, nonprofit, etc.) whose services are retained for a specified activity/project during a set time frame. The consultant/independent contractor works independently and is paid according to the terms of their written agreement with the Foundation. The consultant/independent contractor is not an employee of the CDC Foundation.OPEN REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS: Technical Advisor - Laboratory Capacity Building (Blood Lead Testing) The CDC Foundation is seeking a Country Technical Advisor to support laboratories seeking to build or expand capacity to conduct blood lead testing. This role is a part of a broader Lead Poisoning Prevention Initiative working across nine countries to improve governments ability for early detection and monitoring of lead poisoning.
