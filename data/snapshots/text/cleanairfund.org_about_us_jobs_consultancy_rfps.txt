Request for proposals: Seoul urban air quality planning and ecosystem mapping The Clean Air Fund is inviting proposals to support urban air quality planning in Seoul and identify the ecosystem of partners required to support implementation. The assignment will build on engagement undertaken to date with Seoul Metropolitan Government (SMG) and will be delivered in close collaboration with a small Seoul urban air quality working group. It will establish a shared strategic direction, recommend an initial focus for support, and identify the partnerships and delivery arrangements required to move from planning to implementation. Find out more and apply by December 16, 2026.
