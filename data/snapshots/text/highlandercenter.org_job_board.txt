RFP: Facilities Conditions Assessment In 2024 Highlander purchased nine acres of our original campus in Monteagle, TN including the Historic Highlander Folk School Library, King Cabin and other structures with intent to restore the structures and campus to public use. The Highlander Research and Education Center, with support from the African American Cultural Heritage Action fund of the National Trust, is soliciting competitive fixed price proposals from qualified firms/providers to conduct facilities conditions assessments on the Highlander Folk School and King Cabin. The firm/provider will also assess the grounds and landscapes to make preservation recommendations. The maximum contract amount for this project is $65,000. Final contract award decision and announcement will be made by Nov. 1. Bids on this scope of work will be accepted until October 9, 11:59 pm. The full RFP is available at RFP_Highlander Conditions Assessment, including Scope of Work, Minimum Qualifications, Timeline/Budget, Evaluation Criteria and more. Contract On-Call Interpreters Expanding Our Language Justice Interpreter PoolAmerican Sign Language (ASL) • Spanish • Arabic • Farsi • Haitian Creole • Brazilian Portuguese Highlander Research & Education Center is seeking skilled, reliable contract on-call interpreters to support our multilingual and multicultural community across programs, trainings, and events. Interpreters will provide language access on an as-needed basis for remote and occasional in-person engagements.This is not a full-time or regularly scheduled position; hours will vary based on upcoming events and organizational needs. Our Commitment to Language Justice We believe language is a fundamental part of identity, belonging, and power. Our interpreters play a key role in advancing language justice—ensuring that all people can participate fully, communicate authentically, and be heard in the languages they know best. We welcome interpreters who share this commitment and can help create inclusive, equitable spaces for all. Responsibilities Provide accurate, culturally responsive interpretation (consecutive and/or simultaneous as appropriate). Translate written communications, materials, and publications as needed. Support clear communication between staff, participants, and partners. Uphold confidentiality and community-centered values. Be available for flexible, on-call assignments with varying notice. Requirements Demonstrated fluency in English and at least one of the following: American Sign Language (ASL) • Spanish • Arabic • Farsi • Haitian Creole • Brazilian Portuguese. Prior interpreting or translation experience preferred (community, legal, medical, educational, or movement spaces welcome). Alignment with principles of language justice and inclusive communication. Strong interpersonal communication and professionalism. Ability to work virtually; occasional travel may be requested. Compensation Competitive contract rate; hours are as-needed and not guaranteed. To apply, please send a brief introduction, résumé, and language credentials (if applicable) to hrec@highlandercenter.org with the subject line: On-Call Interpreter – [Your Language]. We look forward to expanding our interpreter community and deepening our collective commitment to language justice. Contract On-Call Child Care Workers Seeking Contract, On-Call Child Care Workers Knoxville, TN Area Highlander Research & Education Center is building a small pool of experienced child care workers for contract, on-call/as-needed assignments to support our programs, gatherings, and community events. This is not a full-time or regularly scheduled position; hours will vary based on upcoming events and organizational needs. About the RoleChild care workers will support children of varying ages during meetings, workshops, and retreats—creating safe, engaging, and nurturing spaces so parents and caregivers can fully participate in Highlander activities. Workers will also collaborate closely with Highlander program staff to integrate age-appropriate social justice curriculum and play-based learning that reflects our mission. Responsibilities Provide attentive, developmentally appropriate care to children in group settings. Create a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment aligned with Highlander’s mission and values. Work with program staff to deliver age-appropriate social justice activities, stories, games, and creative learning experiences. Support play, learning, and rest appropriate to each age group. Communicate clearly with staff and caregivers about needs or concerns. Work on a contract, on-call/as-needed basis, with schedules determined by event timing. Requirements Prior experience in child care, early childhood settings, youth programs, or related fields. Commitment to safety, inclusivity, and community care. Ability to support simple, age-appropriate social justice concepts through storytelling, art, play, and guided activities. Must successfully complete a background check. Must provide two or more professional references. Ability to work evenings or weekends as needed (varies by event). Reliable transportation to Knoxville-area locations. Compensation Competitive contract rate; hours are as-needed and not guaranteed. To apply, please send a brief introduction, résumé, and references to hrec@highlandercenter.org with the subject line: Contract On-Call Child Care Worker.
