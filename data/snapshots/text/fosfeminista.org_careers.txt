Our comprehensive compensation package for each of our hiring locations is competitive with local markets for international NGOs and promotes internal equity in benefits. Specific benefit packages will vary across hiring locations in accordance with relevant labor laws and practice. Except where mandated by applicable laws, any of the core or other Fòs Feminista benefits may be changed, modified, or terminated in accordance with Fòs Feminista’s financial position and the costs of providing such benefits. Salaries Fòs Feminista provides salaries that are internally equitable and locally competitive, alongside other monetary benefits, to support Team Members to have financial security. Recognizing that salary negotiations disproportionately benefit people with privileges, we have eliminated them at hiring. All new positions will be hired at the salary posted for the position. Paid time off In line with its commitments to equity, care, and transparency, Fòs Feminista provides the following minimum benefits across all hiring locations to create a feminist compensation package that supports all Team Members’ financial care, personal care, and professional growth: Vacation days in accordance with relevant labor laws and practice Minimum of 12 sick days 4 Collective Care Days in December/January Minimum of 12 weeks for parental leave Comprehensive medical insurance and other benefits Fòs Feminista ensures that all Team Members, and their life partner and/or eligible family members, have access to quality private health insurance, including medical, dental, and vision benefits at no cost or minimal cost for employees and their eligible family members. Fòs Feminista also provides life insurance for all Team Members and covers disability insurance premiums as required by applicable laws. Flexible work environment Building from mutual trust, Fòs Feminista supports all Team Members to work virtually from their hiring location with the flexibility to create a schedule that accommodates their personal needs and care responsibilities. 54% of Fòs Feminista team members are based in the Global South Our team is based in 17 countries worldwide, reflecting our commitments as a feminist, Global South-led organization to anchor our work and operations close to the communities at the center of our mission. Our Commitment to Equity Fòs Feminista is an equal opportunity employer dedicated to advancing sexual and reproductive rights and health and dismantling inequities from a feminist intersectional lens. Fòs Feminista provides equal opportunities to all employees and applicants for employment without regard to age, race, color, disability, national origin, religion, creed, gender, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital and partnership status, genetic information, veteran status or any other protected category under federal, state and local law. At Fòs Feminista, we continue to build an inclusive culture that encourages, supports, and celebrates the diverse voices and experiences of our employees. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds, identities, and life experiences, ensuring fair consideration for every candidate. Our Commitment to Safeguarding Fòs Feminista affirms the right of everyone with whom we work or engage to be protected from all forms of emotional, physical or sexual abuse, exploitation, harassment, neglect, or any other form of harm. Safeguarding concerns may be raised through our anonymous reporting platform, EthicsPoint.
