Careers & Internships Our Work Culture At SCSJ, we believe in equity, justice, and community-building. Our desire to make tangible change in the South and beyond extends to our workplace, where we prioritize the need to balance achieving our mission with employee wellbeing, support, and work-life balance. Learn more about our mission and vision. Benefits & Perks SCSJ's benefits include health, dental, and vision insurance, a monthly wellness benefit for qualifying expenses, and participation in the organization's 401(k) plan. We also offer vacation and personal/medical leave. Summer Legal Internships SCSJ offers summer legal internships for law students interested in justice system reform, voting rights, or environmental justice.
