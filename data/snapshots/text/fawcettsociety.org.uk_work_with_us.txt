We’re recruiting to both roles through the same process. You can apply for either part time role individually, or for both as a combined full-time appointment if you meet the requirements of both. You can find out more about each role below, with further details on how the combined application works at the end of this page. Both roles report directly to our Chief Executive and carry significant autonomy. They are senior operational posts rather than Senior Leadership Team roles: we’re looking for people who can take ownership of their area, exercise sound judgement, spot risks and opportunities, and make sure the organisation has the information, systems and processes it needs to make good decisions. Senior Finance Manager Salary: £40,000–£50,000 FTEHours: We anticipate around 0.4 FTE / 15 hours per week on average across the year This role will provide senior professional ownership of Fawcett’s finance function. You’ll lead on financial planning, management information, forecasting, controls, restricted funds, year end and the systems that underpin our finances. You’ll work closely with colleagues on funding budgets and full cost recovery, and help ensure the Chief Executive, Senior Leadership Team and trustees have clear, reliable financial information to support good decisions. We’ve already streamlined and automated much of our routine finance processing. We’re now looking for someone who can bring strong professional judgement, make finance work well for a lean organisation, and know when something needs challenge, closer attention or specialist advice. Read the Senior Finance Manager job pack Senior Operations Manager Salary: £40,000–£50,000 FTEHours: We anticipate around 0.6 FTE / 22.5 hours per week This role will create a clear organisational home for many of the functions that sit behind Fawcett’s day-to-day effectiveness. Your remit will include people processes, governance and compliance, our organisational CRM and wider business systems, contracts and suppliers, risk, business continuity and organisational knowledge. You’ll have support from our Business Support Officer, so this is not about personally carrying every administrative task. It is about ownership: making sure important things are held well, improving what is not working and knowing when specialist or senior input is needed. We also want Fawcett to be a genuinely welcoming, supportive and flexible place to work. Good operations here are not just about compliance or meeting legal minimums; they are about putting our values into practice through clear, thoughtful and workable systems and processes.
