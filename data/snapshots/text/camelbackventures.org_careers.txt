Director of Finance Position Location: Remote position, but must be located in the continental United States. Salary range: $130,000-$150,000 Travel Expectations: Up to 15-20% travel for team events Join Our Team as our new Director of Finance The Role: Camelback Ventures is looking for an experienced nonprofit finance leader to lead the financial strategy, management, and systems that support our mission and long-term sustainability. This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who can move comfortably between strategy and execution. You will advise the CEO, Leadership Team, and Board while also staying close to the work of budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, accounting, grants, compliance, and financial operations. You will also lead and strengthen the people, systems, and external partnerships that keep Camelback financially strong and prepared for growth. The Person: You are a senior finance leader who is equally comfortable shaping strategy and ensuring the details get done. You bring calm, clarity, and structure to complex environments, and you understand that strong internal infrastructure is not separate from mission impact—it is what makes mission impact sustainable. You are a trusted advisor to executive leadership and a practical partner to staff. You can move from Board-level financial strategy to workflow design, from audit oversight to systems to staff supervision, without losing sight of the bigger picture. You lead with sound judgment, transparency, discretion, and care. You know how to build systems that are clear enough to support accountability and flexible enough to support people. You are energized by helping a mission-driven organization operate with greater focus, financial clarity, and internal alignment. What You’ll Own: Financial Planning & Analysis: Lead budgeting, forecasting, cash-flow planning, scenario modeling, budget-to-actual analysis, and financial reporting. Provide Leadership Team, Finance Committee, and Board with clear financial insights to support decision-making. Accounting & Compliance: Oversee accounting operations, including monthly close, reconciliations, payroll, oversight of externally managed investment portfolio, functional expense allocations, SAFE agreements, fiscal sponsorship program, audit management, Form 990, internal controls, and regulatory compliance. Grants & Revenue Management: Lead the financial management of grants, including budgets, spending, compliance, and funder reporting. Oversee revenue accounting and reporting, including restricted and conditional contributions, net asset releases, and earned revenue. Finance Team, Systems & Partnerships: Lead and support the finance team; strengthen financial systems, policies, workflows, controls, and documentation; and manage key external relationships including banking, audit, tax, investment advisors, and other finance partners.
