It seeks written input on vendor capabilities, potential solutions, and indicative cost and delivery timelines. The purpose of the RFI is to gather information that may assist in refining technical specifications or the scope of work, and to help prepare interested vendors for participation in future competitive solicitations. Responses to the RFI do not constitute offers and are not used to pre-qualify vendors.
