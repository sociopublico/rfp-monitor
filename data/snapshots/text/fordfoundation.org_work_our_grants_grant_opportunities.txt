We invest in the individuals, ideas and institutions that address the underlying drivers of inequality and align with our programmatic work around the world. The majority of our grants are identified by the foundation and each program has its own strategy and comprehensive selection process. We do not accept unsolicited grant proposals by mail, email or phone. Below are the limited opportunities that accept grant submissions under specific conditions.
