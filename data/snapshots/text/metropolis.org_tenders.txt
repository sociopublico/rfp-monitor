Select YearAll Years All Years20262025202420232022202120202019 12/10/2026 Call for tenders: Terms of Reference for the implementation of the new design and the technical evolution of the Metro Futures platform The Metropolis General Secretariat is launching a call for tenders to select a qualified developer, studio or team to implement the new design and lead the technical evolution of Metro Futures, Metropolis’ urban data and knowledge platform. Developed in 2024 with the support of the Metropolitan Area of Barcelona, Metro Futures brings together comparable indicator data, strategic policy documents, analysis and editorial content from across the Metropolis membership. This new phase, aims to connect these materials so that data leads to the strategies behind it, and strategies lead to the cities and evidence that support them. The selected provider will restructure the platform around a connected content model with topics as the main entry point, implementing a design already defined through a UX/UI review and applying Metropolis’ visual identity. The assignment covers the architecture and data model linking topics, indicators, cities, strategies and evidence; topic-led navigation; comparison and evolution views; the redesign of the foresight toolkit; and full responsiveness and accessibility, as well as technical documentation and training for the Metropolis team. Bidders may also propose an optional conversational assistant that answers from the Metro Futures corpus. The work will take place between October and December 2026. Proposals must be submitted in English by 12 October 2026 to policy@metropolis.org. Please refer to the complete Terms of Reference document for more details on the proposal and specifications. Read call document (opens in a new tab) 12/06/2026 ToR: Logistics and event management for an energy transition event (MGET) *Deadline Extended* The Metropolis Secretariat General is launching a call for tenders to identify a qualified professional event management company to oversee the full logistical, technical, and operational organisation of an upcoming two-day event expected to take place in October 2026 in Dakar, Senegal. The event, a professional fair on the theme of Energy Efficiency, is expected to welcome over 200 participants, and will mark the end of the Metropolitan Governance Energy Transition Project (MGET), a 3-year project led by Metropolis in partnership with the City of Dakar and supported by the European Union. The selected service provider will be responsible for securing and managing the event venue, designing and implementing the spatial layout (including exhibition stands and plenary areas), and ensuring all technical requirements (sound, lighting, equipment, and connectivity) are met. The assignment also includes comprehensive operational management of participants and exhibitors, coordination of the event programme, and implementation of communication activities and materials as defined by Metropolis. In addition, the provider will ensure overall coordination, including timeline planning, stakeholder engagement, and subcontractor management, while guaranteeing full compliance with administrative procedures, safety standards, and risk management protocols. The objective is to deliver a seamless, professional, and high-quality event aligned with Metropolis guidelines. Please refer to the complete Terms of Reference document (in French) for more details on the proposal and specifications. Read call document (opens in a new tab) OBA Ingénierie & Conseils11/05/2026 ToR: Technical Assistance MGET The Metropolis General Secretariat is issuing a call for tenders for technical assistance to support the implementation of the Metropolitan Governance Energy Transition Project (MGET). The MGET project, led by Metropolis in partnership with the City of Dakar and supported by the European Union, is entering a regional phase aimed at building on these lessons learned and supporting five metropolitan areas and coastal cities in West Africa through a structured process of joint assessment and project development for energy initiatives. The project will be awarded to a single vendor to ensure methodological consistency and continuity between the diagnostic and financial structuring phases. The aim is to establish a common methodological framework to ensure that projects are presented in a consistent and comparable manner at the regional level. In addition to the standardized regional portfolio, the contractor must develop a concise format for presenting projects (pitch deck).
