That commitment is true for our staff, members, and vendors with whom we engage. We strive to ensure our vendor engagements are representative of our core values and our charge to embed equity in all processes; both internally and externally. Ensuring we have a transparent process that allows for equal opportunity, to include partnerships with businesses that are owned and operated by women, LGBTQ+ people, Black people and POC, people with disabilities, veterans, and unions- will support the furthering of our mission. We encourage vendors who have traditionally been unrepresented in the reproductive rights movement to submit a proposal to engage in our work.
