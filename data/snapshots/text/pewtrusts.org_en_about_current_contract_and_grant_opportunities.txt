Current Contract and Grant Opportunities Current Contracts and Grants Whether it is improving policy, tracking trends, or bettering life in our hometown of Philadelphia, The Pew Charitable Trusts seeks tangible results through a rigorous, analytical, and evidence-based approach. The foundation of our work is careful planning; a consistent focus on facts, science, and data; strong partnerships; and a commitment to innovation and measurable results. To achieve our goals, we often seek grantmaking opportunities or look to hire a variety of organizations with expertise in one of our fields of interest. Below are our current opportunities: Artificial Intelligence and Public Health Data Advisory Group Type: RFP The Pew Charitable Trusts is seeking proposals from qualified organizations to help develop and launch an advisory group focused on the responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI) to strengthen the quality and analysis of public health data. The selected respondent will work with Pew to identify gaps that prevent the sound use of AI in strengthening public health data and will build consensus on recommendations to address those gaps. The advisory group will bring together a range of perspectives, including those of state public health agencies; health information technology vendors; and experts in AI, data privacy and security, and technology policy. The work will include developing the advisory group structure and composition, launching and managing the group, supporting consensus on guiding principles, and creating a plan to sustain the group over time. The anticipated period of performance is 18 months from the agreement start date.
