With programs in nearly 70 countries and a staff of nearly 100 nationalities, the Institute offers opportunities to grow within the organization, and to learn from the diverse experience of our international staff.NDI's success is a result of the passion, ingenuity, and commitment of our employees worldwide. Our staff of approximately 1,200 works with people across the globe to support and strengthen democratic institutions and practices - helping citizens to monitor their elections; training youth and women to take their first political steps; and assisting parliaments, political parties and civic organizations to instill more transparency and accountability in government.If you share our belief in the vital role that democracy plays in enhancing social and economic well-being and protecting human rights, we encourage you to consider a career or consultancy with NDI. NDI’s career opportunities along with short-term and long-term consultant opportunities can be found under Current Openings. For full-time positions, please submit your application to the position of interest. For consultancies, please follow the instructions in the job posting to submit your resume/CV. If you’d like to be part of our consultant roster, please submit your resume to the generic “Consultant Registry” posting.
